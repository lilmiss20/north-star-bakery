Our Story

A Neighborhood Bakery

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: make honest, comforting baked goods and give neighbors a place to gather. We care about the small moments, from a quick coffee-break pastry to the cake at a family celebration.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We focus on seasonal ingredients, thoughtful recipes, and working with local suppliers when available. Ask our team if you need ingredient details; recipes and availability can change.

Meet the Team

Our Bakers

The early-morning baking team shapes the dough, prepares pastries, and makes sure each tray is ready for the day.

Our Front Counter Crew

Our counter team helps you pick a treat, plan a pickup, and find answers to questions about products and ingredients.

A Note About Allergies

Some products may contain wheat, milk, eggs, nuts, or other allergens. Our shared kitchen may present cross-contact risks. Please describe allergies when contacting us; we cannot guarantee an allergen-free product.
